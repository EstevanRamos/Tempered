# ValueList

The brand's signature device: a stacked list of virtues in uppercase `eyebrow` type, right-aligned in the corner of a hero or story band. Items are `ink-muted`; the last one is lit in `ink` as the conclusion.

Inputs: `items` (3–6 single words or short phrases, ending on the resolution: "A higher standard", "Freedom") and `align`. Use at most once per viewport and never as navigation.
