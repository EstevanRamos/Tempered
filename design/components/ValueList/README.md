# ValueList

A stacked list of virtues in uppercase `eyebrow` type, right-aligned in the corner of the homepage hero, beside a `line-strong` hairline. Items are `ink-muted`; the last one is lit in `ink` as the conclusion ("A higher standard"). On a phone it folds into one wrapped line under the hero's buttons, in `ink-faint`.

Inputs: `items` (3–6 single words or short phrases, ending on the resolution) and `align`. The hero is its only home: where the virtues are the subject of a band, use [TheCode](../TheCode/README.md). Never navigation.
