# ProductCard

A 4:5 image well on `surface` above one line of meta: product name in uppercase `label` on the left, `price` in `ink-muted` on the right. The whole card is one link; the image scales 3% on hover.

Inputs: `name`, `price` (preformatted, e.g. "$34.00"), `image` (product shot on a dark seamless ground, garment centred), `href`, and optionally `badge` + `badgeTone` or `soldOut`.

Lay out 4 across on desktop (`space-12` gap for editorial pages, `space-6` for dense grids), 2 across on mobile. **Don't** add add-to-cart buttons, ratings or swatches to the card — the product page does that work.
