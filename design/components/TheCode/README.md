# TheCode

The brand's virtues, as a code of conduct: five items, each a Roman numeral, a one-word title and one line. It replaces the ValueList wherever the virtues are the subject of a band (the homepage, Our Story); the hero keeps its ValueList.

- Head: the eyebrow ("The code") on the left and, optionally, a muted [TextLink](../TextLink/README.md) on the right ("Our philosophy", omitted on the page it points to). `space-12` beneath on desktop, `space-6` on a phone.
- Numerals: `numeral-l` (76px) from 1024px, `numeral` (44px) below, in Bodoni Moda at optical size 96, drawn as a 0.8px `gold-deep` stroke with no fill. The five numerals together are **the view's one gold element**; nothing else gold may share their view.
- Title in `label` (`eyebrow` on a phone), `ink`; the line in `body`, `ink-faint`.
- Each item sits under a `line` hairline. Five columns with a `space-6` gap from 1024px; below, a list with the numeral in a 64px column beside the title and line.

Numerals are decoration (`aria-hidden`); the list is an ordered list, so the order is still announced. Keep the copy in the source, in the brand voice.
