# QuoteBand

Closes the brand content: the crowned elephant in moving gold foil, the line, the sign-off. Centred on `ground`, `space-24` above and below (`space-16` on a phone), `space-8` between its parts (`space-6` on a phone).

- Crest: `tempered-mark.svg` used as a mask over a `gold-deep` gradient with an `ink` highlight that sweeps across it (the **crest shimmer**, see [Motion](../Motion/README.md)). 256px wide from 1024px, 144px below. It is **the band's one gold element**.
- Line: `quote-l`, italic, sentence case, in curly quotes, balanced, at most 18em wide.
- Sign-off: `eyebrow`, `ink-muted` ("Play with purpose"). Not gold.

No glow behind it: the crest is enough. Use it once per page, and keep The Code out of its view.
